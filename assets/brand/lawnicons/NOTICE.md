In this app, the SVG path commands are mechanically converted to cached Flutter
Path objects without changing their geometry. Stroke color is theme-adapted,
and the original 192-unit viewport and 12-unit round stroke are preserved.
The optional tonal display container is app UI, not part of the imported SVG.

These are community themed brand symbols, not Google-authored brand icons or
an endorsement by Tencent, NetEase, Google, or the Lawnchair project.
